# Open Study

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

**把视频和帖子变成 AI 能用的参考资料。**

将链接发给 AI，让它阅读内容、整理重点，或结合其中的教程和案例，继续帮你完成手头的工作。整理过的内容会保存在资料库，之后可以随时查找、提问和引用。

[开始使用](https://study.faroapi.cn/) · [使用文档](https://docs.study.faroapi.cn/) · [下载插件](https://github.com/moonlight-code-space/open-study/releases/latest) · [加入官方群](https://qm.qq.com/q/1dqW0sSaCY)

### 可以用来做什么

#### 读视频，整理重点

发来一条视频链接，了解它主要讲了什么、有哪些值得参考的做法。需要时，还可以查看字幕、评论和原始来源。

#### 找教程，解决具体问题

学一个新工具、研究一种做法，或者工作进行到一半遇到问题时，让 AI 找些相关教程和案例，通过 Open Study 读取内容，再结合你的情况给出方案。

#### 积累资料，以后接着用

有用的内容不用反复找。保存到资料库后，可以按标题或内容搜索，添加笔记、归入文件夹，也可以导出为 Markdown。

### 你可以这样问

**整理一条链接**

> 帮我读一下这个视频，挑出值得参考的做法和注意事项。
> ［粘贴链接］

**学习新工具**

> 我想用 Python 做一个自动整理表格的小工具。帮我找几个适合新手的实操教程，读完后带我做一个简单版本。

**研究产品**

> 帮我整理这几个产品评测，看看它们各自适合谁、有哪些常见问题。区分视频作者的观点和评论区的反馈。
> ［粘贴链接］

**查找以前的资料**

> 我之前存过一个讲阳台收纳的视频，里面提到折叠晾衣架。帮我找出来，整理一下具体做法。

不用记固定的提示词，说清楚你想解决什么问题就好。回答也不必局限于视频总结，可以是操作步骤、方案对比，或对你现有工作的补充。

### 接入你的 AI 工具

Open Study 提供 **Skill 和 MCP**。Skill 帮助 AI 判断什么时候使用、怎样处理资料；MCP 让它实际读取链接、查询资料库和保存笔记。

安装方法：

1. 打开网站的[快速开始](https://study.faroapi.cn/#ai)。
2. 选择你使用的 AI 软件。
3. 复制对应的安装提示词，发给 AI 按说明安装。
4. 按提示重新打开软件，并登录 Open Study 完成授权。
5. 新建对话，发一条链接试试。

提供 Codex、Claude 原生包，以及 Agent Plugins 标准包、Gemini CLI、Antigravity 适配包和安装材料合集。请按所用软件选择，具体安装与更新方法见快速开始页面。

安装 Skill 后，AI 会在设计、规划、学习操作等适合参考的任务中考虑教程和案例。还没要求参考时，它可以先说明资料能帮上什么忙；你已经要求研究或授权主动参考时，就应直接查找、读取，并把结果用于原任务，不反复询问。实际触发仍取决于软件、模型和当前对话；简单修改或明确不需要研究时不打扰。

新链接由 AI 自己的联网搜索找到，Open Study 负责读取和整理内容。

### 支持哪些内容

支持 B 站、抖音、YouTube、TikTok、小红书、快手、微博、X 和 Instagram 的受支持链接。

- **视频**：字幕、评论和基本信息；没有字幕时，可通过语音转写获取文字。
- **帖子与笔记**：正文、评论和基本信息。

不同平台和内容可获取的信息有所差异，实际支持范围与积分规则以网站说明为准。

### 也可以直接在网站使用

不安装插件，也能在[网站](https://study.faroapi.cn/)粘贴链接、整理内容和查看资料。

网站与已连接的 AI 工具共用同一个账号的资料库。在网页上保存的内容，可以让 AI 继续查找和使用。

## English

**Turn videos and posts into reference material for your AI agent.**

Send your AI a link to read, summarize, or use as a reference for the task you are working on. Content you save stays in your library, ready to search, ask questions about, and cite later.

[Website](https://study.faroapi.cn/) · [Documentation (Chinese)](https://docs.study.faroapi.cn/) · [Download the plugin](https://github.com/moonlight-code-space/open-study/releases/latest) · [Feedback](https://github.com/moonlight-code-space/open-study/issues)

### What you can do

#### Read a video and find the useful parts

Share a video link to find out what it covers and which ideas are worth trying. You can also check the transcript, comments, and original source.

#### Find tutorials for a specific problem

When you are learning a tool, exploring an approach, or stuck on a task, ask your AI to find relevant tutorials and examples. Open Study reads the content so the AI can use it to help with your situation.

#### Build a library you can return to

Save useful content instead of searching for it again. Search your library by title or content, add notes, organize items into folders, or export them as Markdown.

### Things to ask your AI

**Read a link**

> Read this video and pick out the practical ideas and things to watch out for.
> [Paste a link]

**Learn a new tool**

> I want to build a small Python tool that organizes spreadsheets automatically. Find a few beginner-friendly, hands-on tutorials, read them, and help me build a simple version.

**Research products**

> Review these product videos. Who is each product suited to, and what problems come up? Keep the creators’ opinions separate from feedback in the comments.
> [Paste links]

**Find something you saved**

> I saved a video about balcony storage that mentioned a folding drying rack. Find it and explain how it was set up.

You do not need a fixed prompt. Describe what you want to accomplish. The answer can be a summary, a set of steps, a comparison, or a useful addition to work you have already started.

### Connect your AI tools

Open Study provides a **Skill and an MCP connection**. The Skill guides the AI on when and how to use the material. MCP lets it read links, search your library, and save notes.

To get started:

1. Open [Quick start](https://study.faroapi.cn/#ai) on the website.
2. Choose the AI app you use.
3. Copy its installation prompt and send it to your AI to follow.
4. Reopen the app when prompted, then sign in to Open Study to authorize the connection.
5. Start a new conversation and try sending a link.

Downloads include native Codex and Claude packages, an Agent Plugins standard package, Gemini CLI and Antigravity adapters, and a bundle of installation materials. Choose the option for your app. Installation and update instructions are available on the Quick start page.

With the Skill installed, your AI considers tutorials and examples for tasks such as design, planning, and learning a practical skill. It may first explain how references would help. Once you have requested research or authorized it to consult references, it should find and read them, then use the findings in your original task without asking again. Actual use depends on the app, model, and conversation. Simple edits and requests to skip research do not need an extra suggestion.

Your AI’s own web search finds new links; Open Study reads and organizes their content.

### Supported content

Open Study supports eligible links from Bilibili, Douyin, YouTube, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, Kuaishou, Weibo, X, and Instagram.

- **Videos:** transcripts, comments, and basic information. Speech transcription can provide text when captions are unavailable.
- **Posts:** post text, comments, and basic information.

Available information varies by platform and item. See the website for current support details and credit usage.

### Use the website without a plugin

You can also paste links, organize content, and read your saved material directly on the [website](https://study.faroapi.cn/).

The website and connected AI tools share the library associated with your account. Content saved on the website is also available to your AI.
